# Auto-tariff-event-study
This study asks whether the US 25% tariff on autos and auto parts, effective April 3, 2025 under Section 232, moved the stock prices of Korean automakers more than those of US automakers. It compares Hyundai (005380.KS) and Kia (000270.KS) with GM and Ford in an event study with a difference-in-differences (DiD) comparison.
